# Better Emails

Use Amtelco's `SendMessage` schedule or script element to save messages to a file, which are then processed and sent by Mission Control.

- **Title** - Shows as the title at the top of the email
- **Description** - Shows underneath the title and as the email preview
- **Logo Url** - A full URL to the logo image
- **Logo Alt Text** - Text to display when the logo can't be loaded
- **Logo Link** - A URL to link the logo to when clicked
- **Button Text** - A call-to-action button text at the bottom of the email
- **Button Link** - Can be any standard URL such as `https://`, `http://`, or `mailto:`
- **Theme** - Choose from a list of pre-defined themes or upload your own custom theme
- **Message History** - Whether to display the message history underneath each message
- **Report Metadata** - Whether to display the report metadata at the top of the email

## CAN-SPAM Compliance

CAN-SPAM is a law that sets the rules for commercial email, establishes requirements for commercial messages, gives recipients the right to have you stop emailing them, and spells out tough penalties for violations.

While it primarily applies in Canada, it's good practice to follow. 

More information can be found here: https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business

- **Deceptive Subject Lines** - It's up to you to make sure your email subject lines are accurate and not misleading
- **Unsubscribe Link** - An unsubscribe link that notifies the call center of the removal for follow-up
- **Physical Address** - The physical address of the call center to be displayed in the footer
